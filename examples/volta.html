Our Story

Crafted for Those
Who Refuse to Choose
Between Work & Pleasure.

Volta was born from a simple frustration: why must great coffee and serious productivity live in separate worlds? We set out to build a space that demands nothing of you — except to do your best work.

Every element is deliberate. The acoustics, the lighting at different hours, the weight of the cups in your hand. We source our beans directly from single-origin farms across the Indonesian archipelago and roast in-house every Monday.

The workspace follows the same philosophy. Ergonomic without being clinical. Quiet without being sterile. A room that breathes with you.
