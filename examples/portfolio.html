I'm a product designer and creative developer based in Jakarta, obsessed with the space where visual craft meets technical precision. I don't just hand off designs — I build them.

My work spans fintech platforms, SaaS dashboards, consumer apps, and brand identities. I care deeply about the edge cases, the micro-interactions, and the moments users don't notice — because those are the ones they feel.
